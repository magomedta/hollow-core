## 1. NARRATIVE PHILOSOPHY: ENVIRONMENTAL ARCHAEOLOGY

Hollow Core rejects exposition dumps, talking NPCs, and cutscene interruptions. The narrative is reconstructed through four physical layers:
1. **Textual Relics:** Fragile historical documents preserved in iron dispatch boxes.
2. **Material Traces:** Interrupted scenes, abandoned workstations, and barricades.
3. **Lithic Resonance:** Acoustic memory trapped in the stone matrix.
4. **Architectural Archaeology:** Shifts from 19th-century timber shoring to 1940s Syndicate iron bulkheads.

## 2. DOCUMENT D-01: THE PROKHOROV LOG (1894)

### Location & Physical Carrier:
Mounted inside a rusted iron dispatch box on the west wall of Airlock Station 4 ($X: 12.5, Y: 39.4, Z: -6.5$).

### Diegetic Presentation:
Inspecting the box opens the `DocumentViewer`—a weathered, yellowed parchment sheet with aged ink, Cyrillic period typography, and the seal of Artel "North".

### Complete Canonical Text:
> **ВАХТЕННЫЙ ЖУРНАЛ ШУРФА №7 (АРТЕЛЬ 'СЕВЕР')**  
> *1894 г. // ГОРИЗОНТ IV // ЗАБОЙ 4-Б*  
>
> *«14 ноября. Забой 4-Б сухой. Артельщик Прохоров пожаловался, что порода перед ним вздыхает. Наложил штраф в полтинник за суеверие.*  
>
> *16 ноября. Прохоров не вышел. Нашли его в штреке: сидит на коленях, прижав ухо к гранитному забою. Зубы сжаты так, что лопнули резцы. Шепчет: 'Она зовет меня по имени-отчеству'. Отправили наверх в лазарет.*  
>
> *18 ноября. Лазарет сообщает: Прохоров ночью разбил окно и сбежал обратно вниз. Без сапог и фонаря. В стволе №2 слышали его смех. Мы заложили ствол дубовым брусом на шесть клиньев».*

### Narrative Significance:
This document establishes the foundational lore theme: stone is not an inert mineral, but an acoustic medium capable of holding voices and inducing auditory hallucinations.

## 3. EASTER EGG EE-06: «УТОНУВШИЙ СЕЙФ СИНДИКАТА 104»

### Lore Origin:
During the rapid evacuation of the Syndicate 104 mining consortium in 1942, chief accountant Brandt locked emergency reserves and sensitive acoustic blueprints inside a waterproof safe in the lower drainage collector.

### Discovery Sequence:
1. **Drainage Requirement:** The sump at $X: 14, Y: 28, Z: -12$ is flooded. Turning the heavy cast-iron gate valve wheel at Airlock Station 4 drains the sump.
2. **Combination Lock:** A 4-digit code dial requires combination `1-9-2-4` (scratched into a nearby timber beam alongside miner tallies).
3. **Unlocking Reward:**
   - 6 Syndicate Gold Tokens.
   - 1 Brass Tuning Fork (calibrated to the 11.4 Hz Lithic Resonance frequency).
   - Blueprint for the Watertight Submersible Lamp.
4. **Paranormal Repercussion:**
   - The player's lantern flickers violently for 3.2 seconds.
   - On the distant abyss ledge across the chasm ($X: -15, Y: 32, Z: -12$), thirty meters away in the swirling fog, appears the immobile silhouette of **The Watcher** with twin cold white pin-prick eyes.
   - Approaching or focusing the lantern beam causes the entity to dissolve into basalt dust accompanied by a deep, decaying organ chord.

## 4. MATERIAL TRACES IN THE ABANDONED CAMP (ZONE D)
