## 1. Architectural History of Horizon 1420m

The vertical slice represents the intersection of natural subterranean caverns and 19th-century industrial excavation:

### 1.1 Timber Descent Gallery (Zone A)
- **Construction:** Heavy square hewn timber frames (`HEWN_TIMBER`, $X = 0, 3, 6, 9, 12$).
- **Function:** Stabilized the treacherous permafrost crevasse against glacial shifting.
- **Narrative Detail:** Severed telegraph cables dangling from wall anchors; painted depth marker stencil ("1420m") at $X = 8, Y = 50$.

### 1.2 Airlock Station 4 (Zone B)
- **Construction:** Heavy load-bearing slate-brick barrel vaulting (`SLATE_BRICK`).
- **Key Installations:**
  - **Surveyor Workstation:** Desk with Document D-01 ("Журнал Станции 4: Аномалии Эха"), emergency lantern, ration crates.
  - **Drainage Sluice Channel:** 2.8m-wide floor trench with flowing subterranean water.
  - **Cast-Iron Drainage Valve:** Wall-mounted handwheel at $X = 16.2, Y = 39.5, Z = -12.0$. Rotating the valve drains the lower sump chamber.

### 1.3 Abandoned Expedition Camp (Zone D)
- **Construction:** Natural cavern grotto with timber tent frames and supply crates.
- **Narrative Role:** Designated **Safe Refuge** registered with `AnomalyDirector.add_safe_zone()`. No psychological anomalies or apparitions trigger inside this warm haven.

### 1.4 Drowned Sump & Safe EE-06 (Zone E)
- **Construction:** Deep drainage collector vault at $Y = 28$.
- **Objective:** Submerged under water until the player opens the Station 4 valve; contains the expedition strongbox with the surveyor's final tape and journal.
