## 1. Flora & Fungi Classification

Subterranean ecosystems lack solar irradiance. Vegetation relies on geothermal warmth, moisture, and chemical synthesis:

### 1.1 Cyan Bioluminescent Mushrooms (`BIOLUM_MUSHROOM` / Block ID 25)
- **Biome:** Native to **Базальтовая Абиссаль (Zone C)**.
- **Ecological Role:** Symbiotic colonizers on damp basalt rock faces and mineral terraces.
- **Physical Appearance:** Clustered caps with pale cyan-turquoise bio-photoluminescence.
- **Lighting Emission:** Casts a soft ambient cyan glow ($R = 9\text{m}..16\text{m}$, Color `Color(0.18, 0.90, 0.82)`), revealing the scale of the chasm in the darkness.
- **Locations in M1:**
  - High Overlook Ledge ($X = 3..4, Y = 34, Z = -21..-27$).
  - Intermediate Basalt Terrace ($X = -7..-4, Y = 27, Z = -27..-24$).
  - Deep Chasm Floor ($X = -2..0, Y = 23, Z = -28..-22$).

### 1.2 Subglacial Calcite Formations (`CALCITE` / Block ID 11)
- **Biome:** Dominates **Ледниковые Шпили (Zone A)**.
- **Ecological Role:** Glistening crystalline stalactites and icicles formed by slow mineral percolation through permafrost fractures.
- **Acoustic Characteristic:** High acoustic reflection, producing sharp glassy echoes.

### 1.3 Hanging Lichen Roots (`LICHEN_ROOT` / Block ID 29)
- **Biome:** Ceiling fissures of the **Базальтовая Абиссаль (Zone C)** ($Y = 42..43$).
- **Visual Function:** Fibrous tendrils reaching downward from moisture-rich fissures.
